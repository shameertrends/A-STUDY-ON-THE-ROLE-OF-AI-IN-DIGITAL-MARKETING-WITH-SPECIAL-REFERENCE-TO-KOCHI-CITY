# A-STUDY-ON-THE-ROLE-OF-AI-IN-DIGITAL-MARKETING-WITH-SPECIAL-REFERENCE-TO-KOCHI-CITY
This research investigates the transformative impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on digital marketing practices in Kochi, India, focusing on its adoption, benefits, challenges, and consumer perceptions, with an emphasis on small and medium enterprises (SMEs).
